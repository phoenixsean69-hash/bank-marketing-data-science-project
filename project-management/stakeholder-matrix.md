# Stakeholder Matrix

## Project: Predicting Customer Response to Bank Marketing Campaigns

| Stakeholder | Type | Main Interest | Influence | Interest Level | Main Expectation | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing Manager | Internal | Improve campaign targeting | High | High | Useful and understandable predictions | Regular progress review and final results discussion |
| Marketing Staff | Internal | Reduce unnecessary customer contacts | Medium | High | Clear indication of which customers may respond | Demonstration and practical feedback |
| Bank Management | Internal | Improve campaign efficiency and control cost | High | Medium | Evidence of business value | Milestone summary and final report |
| Data Analyst | Internal | Produce a reliable and reproducible model | High | High | Good-quality data and clear objectives | Continuous technical review |
| IT / Data Support | Internal | Ensure data and technical processes are manageable | Medium | Medium | Secure and reproducible solution | Technical consultation where needed |
| Customers | External | Fair and responsible use of their information | Low individually | High | Privacy, fairness and reduced unnecessary contact | Consider privacy and fairness during analysis |

## Power-Interest Classification

### Manage Closely
- Marketing Manager
- Data Analyst

### Keep Satisfied
- Bank Management

### Keep Informed
- Marketing Staff
- Customers

### Monitor
- IT / Data Support where no deployment is required

## Possible Stakeholder Conflicts

A possible conflict exists between the marketing team's desire to contact customers who are most likely to subscribe and customers' interest in privacy and avoiding excessive marketing contact.

Another possible conflict is between management's desire for a highly accurate model and the project's limited time and resources.

These conflicts will be handled by keeping the model simple, documenting limitations and ensuring that predictions are treated as decision-support information rather than automatic instructions.
